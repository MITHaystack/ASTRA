## ASTRA Safety

Generally ASTRA is very safe. Weather conditions for outdoor use should be
considered. So dressing appropriately for the conditions is the most important 
factor. Although ASTRA doesn't move too fast it is good to give it some distance 
when the antenna is moving. Don't open and service the electronics unless you
know you are qualified. 

Protecting ASTRA is also important. The system has been used from very cold to very hot
temperatures. It will shut down if it overheats which can happen in direct sunlight during
daytime observations. The system itself uses normal electrical power from a battery. 
It isn't waterproof so avoid getting the hardware wet. Wipe dry if condesation becomes
an issue. Care carefully for the optical elements and clean them per [best practices](https://www.cloudynights.com/forums/topic/741696-directions-for-cleaning-telescope-optics/) for
optical telescopes. 

**For daytime observations. Don't look through the optics or use the imager when pointing at the Sun without a solar filter!**

### Don't Hurt Yourself

1. Never Open ASTRA Enclosures While Powered
1. Stay Clear of ASTRA while it moves
1. Dress For the Weather
1. Don't Use During Rain 
1. Go Inside During Lightning
1. Avoid the Sun without a solar filter in place
1. Move ASTRA in sections or roll the base, don't lift the whole thing
1. Ensure the leveling feet are down prior to turning the system on
1. Turn ASTRA off when not in use, ensure the battery is off

### Don't Hurt ASTRA

1. Don't drop or hit the enclosures
1. Be careful with cables and wraping during motion
1. Don't move the mount without watching (i.e. in person or via camera)
1. Avoid touching the optical elements
1. Cover the imaging sensor opening if removed
1. Cover the telescope lens when not in use (lens cap)
1. Don't bend or torque the antenna
1. Don't break the antenna feed or calibration injector
1. Be careful that the hardware doesn't roll or flip during transport
1. Never roll the mount without the tripod tied down
1. Ensure screws and bolts are tight before and after relocating
1. Shade the computer and battery when in direct sunlight (i.e. a parasol will do)
1. Open top cover of battery and DAQ enclosure when in hot weather
1. Store ASTRA in a temperature stable location
