## ASTRA Environmental 

ASTRA should generally be kept dry. We have operated the system over a fairly
wide temperature and humidity range. However, a lot of inexpensive
parts are used and in particular the computers can turn off if they overheat.
This is most common in direct sunlight during daytime observations of the Sun. 
We recomend opening the lid during such observations and also using a parasol or
other shade to reflect sunlight away from the DAQ and Energy unit enclosure.
